Eligibility

  • Applicants must be authorised representatives of a Lyrovian national, Hergwel citizen, or registered entity.
  • Requests must be linked to a defined statutory purpose.

Jurisdiction selection

Current: Hegemonic State of Lyrophia Auctoritate Imperii Immediata

What you need

  • Verified reference number or prior receipt.
  • Signed authority from the subject individual.
  • Organisation seal or audit officer confirmation.

Steps

  1. Submit applicant and subject details.
  2. Upload statutory purpose declaration.
  3. Receive acknowledgement and verification report.
